# Admin guide

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows total bookings, upcoming jobs, pending jobs, completed jobs and revenue.

## Booking management

Search by customer or booking reference. Filter by status. Assign another professional from the staff dropdown or mark a job complete/cancelled.

## Staff

Staff hours are calculated from non-cancelled bookings in the current Monday–Sunday week. Add staff members directly from the dashboard.

## Settings

Change company name, logo URL and hourly prices. These values feed the public booking site and the booking pricing function.

## Export

Use Export CSV to download the current booking dataset for reporting or accounting.
